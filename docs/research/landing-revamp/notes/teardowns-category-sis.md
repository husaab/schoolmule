# Category teardowns: SIS and school-management vendor websites (captured 2026-10-25)

Scope: the marketing sites a principal of a 30–200-student independent JK–8 school in Ontario is likely to compare SchoolMule against. Homepages and up to two extra pages per vendor were captured with `tools/shoot.mjs` (desktop 1440×900 fold, desktop full page, iPhone-class 390×844 @2x fold and full page, plus a JSON of title/meta/headings/nav/CTAs/video use). All screenshot paths below are relative to `docs/research/landing-revamp/`. Visual claims are from screenshots that were opened and looked at. Text claims are from the captured JSON or from WebFetch of the same URL. Nothing was submitted, signed up for or booked.

Capture notes:
- The capture locale was en-CA and the timezone America/Toronto, and the machine is in Canada. Several sites changed what they showed for Canada. Veracross opened a Canada popup, FACTS preselected "Canada" in its country picker, and Gradelink's phone field defaulted to the Canadian flag.
- Some full-page desktop captures show blank regions below the fold, because the content only renders when scrolled into view (FACTS, PowerSchool, Blackbaud K-12, Alma). The text for those regions was taken from WebFetch.
- Edsby's site returned a bot-check page ("Checking your browser… / Confirm you are human", HTTP 403) to the headless browser, so its visuals were not captured. Its text came from WebFetch. No attempt was made to get around the bot check.
- maplewoodsoftware.com was down. It timed out, returned a 502 and refused connections on 2026-10-25.
- Two vendors have rebranded. sycamoreschool.com now redirects to sycamoreleaf.com ("Sycamore Leaf Solutions"), and edsembli.com redirects to sparkrock.com/k12/.
- Because Maplewood had no live site, a 12th vendor was added: **MySchool (trymyschool.com)**. It is a CIS Ontario sponsor whose headline is literally "Built For Canadian Schools", which makes it the closest positioning overlap with SchoolMule found in this research. SchoolCues is the small-school, faith-school pick.

## How does each SIS-category vendor's website sell, and what should SchoolMule borrow or avoid?

### Takeaway
Almost every vendor gates the product behind a sales demo. Only Sycamore ($4/student/month, a full pricing page) and SchoolCues ("as low as $75.00/mo") publish prices. None shows an Ontario report card, the OEN, or a JK–8 principal persona. The incumbents (Veracross, Blackbaud, FACTS, PowerSchool) lead with abstract platform or AI claims and big counts. The two sites that speak most directly to SchoolMule's buyer are MySchool, which is Canada-first and shows provincial reporting, Canadian hosting and CAD invoicing as a card grid, and Edsby, whose social proof is dense with Ontario schools but never says "Ontario". The open ground for SchoolMule is a combination nobody offers: small-school Ontario specificity, a visible price, and real product UI on a phone-first fold.

## What do small schools say about each vendor's price and complexity on G2 and Capterra? (light pass)

### Takeaway
The pattern is consistent across reviews: the big suites (Blackbaud, Veracross, PowerSchool) are rated capable but complex and costly for small schools, while the small-school tools (SchoolCues, Gradelink, Sycamore) are praised for affordability and support and criticised for dated or thin features. G2 and Capterra pages returned 403 to WebFetch, so every rating and quote below comes from search-engine result summaries of those pages and should be re-checked before being quoted publicly.

### Cited Findings
- **Veracross:** G2 4.3/5 (37 reviews) and Capterra 4.3/5 (13), with Capterra value for money at 3.9/5, its lowest sub-score. A G2 reviewer notes "the learning curve can be steep for new users". One cost complaint is that two-factor authentication costs extra. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/veracross); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/20395/Veracross/reviews/)
- **Blackbaud K-12:** Software Advice lists a "starting price of $21,000.00 per year". Capterra shows value for money 3.7/5 and ease of use 3.4/5. One reviewer calls it a "very capable behemoth". — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/k-12/blackbaud-k-12-profile/); [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/196276/k-12-schools)
- **FACTS:** A Capterra reviewer calls it "clunky… too many clicks necessary to complete basic tasks". An administrative assistant at a small school chose it for cost but says it "isn't the most robust system". — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148464/FACTS/reviews?page=2)
- **Gradelink:** Capterra about 4.7 (642). A sample tier is listed at "$114 per month for up to 50 students". Support is praised. The cons are an "outdated and unintuitive interface" and no Teams sync. — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/118900/Gradelink-SIS/); [G2 review](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/gradelink-review-1546245)
- **Sycamore:** Capterra 4.4 (55). A 2019 reviewer says it doesn't charge extra for add-ons, "a good fit for small or non-profit schools", but criticises the thin online documentation. — [Capterra CA](https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/75048/sycamore-education)
- **SchoolCues:** Capterra about 4.7 (93–96). Small schools call it affordable. The cons are that the mobile app lacks message notifications and feels "slow and laggy", and that less tech-comfortable teachers struggled. One reviewer moved from FACTS to get everything in one app. Capterra also lists pricing "as low as $1.00/child/month", which differs from the site's "$75/mo". — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/206168/SchoolCues/reviews/); [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/206168/schoolcues)
- **Alma:** Capterra 4.6 (451). The cons are gaps in fees and invoicing (one school uses Stripe instead) and slowness "during high-traffic periods". — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/133283/Alma/reviews/)
- **Edsby:** Capterra 4.3 (148) and G2 4.5 (111). Parents praise it. The cons are messaging organisation (no thread or rename) and sync and transcript issues with the school database. — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/122855/Edsby/reviews/)
- **Toddle:** About 4.7 on a small sample. The cons include frequent interface changes and not being able to copy a class. — [Software Advice AU](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/503683/Toddle)
- **PowerSchool:** Capterra 4.3 (143). A small school wrote "Good but too much for us… too difficult for a small school" and switched to a simpler system. A district notes "a lot of unknown costs that come along with the base product". — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/154883/PowerSchool-Student-Information-System/reviews/)
- **Edsembli:** The Capterra US listing shows 0 user reviews. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/219314/Edsembli-SIS/)
- **MySchool:** Gartner Peer Insights 3.0 from 2 reviews. Capterra and GetApp show 4.7 from 34 users. — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/myschool); [Capterra CA](https://www.capterra.ca/software/114989/myschool)
- **Competitor-sourced pricing (biased):** Sycamore claims "Blackbaud, TADS, and Veracross… reported by schools at $20K–$28K+/year" and "FACTS lists modular pricing starting at $5–6/student/month". — [sycamoreleaf.com/school-k-12](https://sycamoreleaf.com/school-k-12/)

### Inferences
- "Too much for a small school" is SchoolMule's wedge against the suites. "Dated and thin" is its wedge against the cheap tools. The site can make both contrasts without naming anyone ("built for 30–200 students: not a district system cut down, not a newsletter app built up").

### Gaps
- Review texts were not read in full because G2 and Capterra block fetches. The numbers are from search summaries and could be stale or slightly misquoted.
- No Canadian-specific review commentary was found for any vendor.
